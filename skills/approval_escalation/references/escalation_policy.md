# Corporate Approval Escalation Policy Reference

## Tiered Escalation Matrix
- **Standard Expenses (< $5,000):** Escalate to primary backup approver after 72 hours.
- **High-Value Expenses (>= $5,000):** Escalate to Department Director or Corporate Admin.
- **Fail-Closed Fallback:** When no designated backup approver exists, route strictly to the configured `admin_fallback_email`.
